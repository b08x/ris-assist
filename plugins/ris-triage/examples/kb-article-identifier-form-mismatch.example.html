Study not found when searching PACS with an accession copied from the raw HL7 order

KB[TBD] | v1.0 | 2026-08-07

🛠️ Introduction

A recognition aid, not a resolution procedure. A "study not found" report may be an identifier form mismatch rather than a missing study — rule this out before treating it as a delivery or interface failure.

  • Applies to: integration engine, RIS, PACS
  • STOP conditions: this article does not resolve anything. No incident underlies it and no fix is recorded; it exists to stop a lookup failure being misrouted as a delivery failure.

🔎 Step 1: Confirm the symptom and scope

The accession number carried in the raw HL7 order message and the accession displayed in the RIS and PACS are not the same string. The raw message carries an unprefixed sequence; downstream systems display the site-prefixed form defined in the site profile.

Raw HL7 orderDisplayed in RIS / PACS
Accession40551827IMG40551827
  • A PACS search using a value copied from the raw message returns nothing, and the reverse also fails. This presents as "the study doesn't exist" when the study does exist.
  • Scope observed: not user-scoped — applies to identifier lookups generally.
  • Confidence: confirmed — read directly from both systems side by side.
